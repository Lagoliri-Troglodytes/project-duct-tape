Game Title: Project Duct Tape
Date Created: April 12th, 2026
Last Date Modified: April 30th, 2026
Godot Version: 4.6.2


Project Duct Tape is a traditional 2D platformer where the player uses duct tape to patch rifts and defeat enemies. When all rifts in a level are patched, the player can return to the start of the level and drive away.


Platformer Controls:

Keyboard:
W, A, D - jump, move left, move right
Space - attack

Controller:
Left Stick/D-Pad Left, Right - move left, move right
Bottom Action Button - jump
Right Action Button - attack


UI Navigation Controls:

Keyboard:
Mouse, Left Click: free movement, click buttons
WASD, Arrow Keys - up, left, down, right movement
Enter/Space - confirm

Controller:
Left Stick/D-Pad - up, down, left, right movement
Action Buttons - confirm


Win/Lose Conditions:

Win:
If the player patches up all rifts and returns to the car at the beginning of the level within the time limit, they can drive away and win.

Lose:
If the player fails to patch up all rifts and return to the car before the timer reaches zero, they will lose. If the player loses all of their health by taking damage from enemies, they will also lose.
